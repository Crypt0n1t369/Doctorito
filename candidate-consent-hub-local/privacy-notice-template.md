## Why we process candidate information

If you choose to join the candidate shortlist, we process your CV and professional-profile information to identify possible employment opportunities in the energy industry. We do not treat a visit to the consent page, an email open, or a link click as consent.

## Your choices

You can separately choose whether we may:

1. use your existing CV and professional profile for opportunity matching;
2. contact you by email about possible matches; and
3. contact you by telephone.

We do not disclose your CV to an employer merely because you joined the shortlist. We will ask separately before disclosure to a specific employer or for a specific opportunity, unless another clearly explained lawful basis applies.
