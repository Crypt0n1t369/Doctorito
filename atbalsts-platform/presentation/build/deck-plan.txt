2. Position Atbalsts under existing Latvian authority.
3. Demonstrate the minimal citizen interaction.
4. Demonstrate the minimal authority interaction.
5. Show the control architecture and AI boundary.
6. Establish exercise-first safety and compliance.
7. Propose a 12-week development and exercise path.
8. Define the evidence gate for any expansion.
