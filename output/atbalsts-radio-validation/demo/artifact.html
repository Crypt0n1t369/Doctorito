Run it with no network — both devices in airplane mode
Checking…

  1. Open this page on both devices and press Save for offline use on each.
  2. Turn on airplane mode on both. The badge above must read offline.
  3. On the sender, tap a shelter, pick a status, press Encode. The bytes that go over the air appear underneath — a status change is five of them.
  4. On the receiver, press Listen with microphone.
  5. Press Transmit, speaker within about 10–30 cm of the receiver.
  6. Watch the receiver. The shelter changes colour on its map and appears in its list — with no radio of any kind switched on.
