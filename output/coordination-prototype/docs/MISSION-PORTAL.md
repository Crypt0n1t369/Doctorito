[VALUE-AND-SCOPE.md](VALUE-AND-SCOPE.md) states the value to test; [CONSTRAINTS.md](CONSTRAINTS.md) retains C1–C9. The [23 September vision](MISSION-PORTAL-2026-09-23.md) is preserved as history. ## What it could enable

A person begins with a worthwhile question and a useful account. A small group helps develop it without losing sources, corrections or responsibility. Specific public contributions become possible when there is capacity to review them. Used results may then attract support for further work. Remaining small can be a successful outcome.

The mission's purpose provides direction. It cannot determine what an investigation finds. Discovering that the premise was wrong, retaining an unresolved alternative or ending the inquiry can be legitimate results. Shared understanding does not require unanimous belief.

A portal takes responsibility for maintaining a record and following up within its mandate. People and institutions retain responsibility for consequential decisions. A project owner cannot manufacture legitimacy for a wider community simply by signing a charter.

## Shared foundation; different decisions

```text
sources and contributions
          |
          v
permission check -> durable draft + source/version references
          |
          v
review -> revised account + reasons + contributor receipt
          |
          +-> inquiry: evidence, uncertainty, alternatives
          +-> collective choice: options, mandate, recorded decision
          +-> execution: accepted work, delivery, result review
          |
          v
explicit release of a selected version -> corrections and next questions
```

The three activities share records but have different authority. Journalism needs editorial verification, research needs method and replication, communities develop practice, and participation needs a real route to decisions. Domain fit can require different workflows and implementation. Avoid a universal trust score.

Permissions apply to sources, derived accounts, retrieval, model processing and release. The public view is a permitted version; it is not a redacted copy of unrestricted private context. Preserve the distinction between a saved draft, reviewed finding, chosen action and published account.

## How support could become capacity

Support can provide time, expertise, equipment, funds or compute. Identify the actual limiting resource first. More submissions can increase delay when review capacity is fixed. Keep ready work bounded by the group's ability to review it; pause intake or narrow invitations when the backlog grows.

Support can influence what receives attention, with disclosed rules. It cannot buy factual conclusions or undisclosed decision rights. Funding, legal custody and wider representative governance are future operating decisions. A supporter count is not evidence of public representation or effective capacity.

For persistent information, preserve permitted lineage and routes to rediscovery. Freshness, archival status, deletion obligations and actual maintenance still matter. An indefinite opportunity to revisit a finding does not require permanent active attention or unlimited retention.

## What the next builder should do

1. Run or facilitate the manual episode in the workbook with a willing lead; preserve the baseline and every observation, including non-use. Internal rehearsal does not establish adoption.
2. If the episode is useful, implement one vertical slice: source and finding saved durably; visible location and audience; revision with reason; contribution review linked to the changed account and a receipt. Demonstrate reopen/restart without losing it. Use explicit human review and keep the model optional.
3. Verify every boundary the slice exercises: recipient-scoped retrieval and derived context, unauthorized reads/exports, revocation during queued work, retry idempotency and consistent review/receipt writes. Existing private paths still need Gate 0 repair. A manual public-document trial does not bypass those requirements for software.
4. Test a fresh-reader handover and repeated use. Compare total upkeep with ordinary documents. Add broader intake only when review capacity supports it.
5. Evaluate general LLM assistance and Jev on the observed tasks, with separate Latvian results. Add training, support allocation or federation only for a demonstrated need and appropriate rights.

The immediate success measure is an inspectable contribution-to-result episode that people choose to repeat. Matching accuracy and model cost remain component measures. The experiment may show that a document template is sufficient; that is useful evidence, not a reason to add more architecture.
