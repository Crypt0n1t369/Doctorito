# Value to test, and the first useful experiment

29 September 2026. Integrated after the user requested experiment suggestions and the lessons to be brought into the project. This is working guidance; no pilot participants, market demand or completed product are claimed. The exact [23 September assessment](VALUE-AND-SCOPE-2026-09-23.md) is retained as historical research, including claims that this update challenges.

## Value hypothesis

Help a person or small group turn scattered contributions into a current, source-linked account, useful next steps and visible results. People should be able to resume, correct and contribute without one coordinator reconstructing everything. A contributor should see what happened to their input.

The comparison is the group's actual documents, notes and task tools. The proposed advantage is a maintained connection between sources, changing understanding, accepted work and outcomes. If maintaining that connection costs more than it saves, simplify or stop. Existing tools may already be sufficient.

## What the critique changes

- Recall tools address parts of memory. They do not establish that continuity, correction and shared understanding are solved, or that solo use has no value.
- AI-heavy work is a possible use case, not a prerequisite. Human research, cultural practice and practical making can suffer the same fragmentation.
- Automatic suggestions and one-click confirmation do not remove review, reconciliation or stale information. Count total human effort.
- A common record, permission and provenance foundation is plausible. Journalism, research, community practice and public decisions can require different acceptance workflows and code.
- Convergence is not always success. Disproving the premise, preserving uncertainty and maintaining an alternative can be useful outcomes.
- Participation, evidence, agreement and authority remain distinct. Additional support helps only if it addresses a real capacity constraint.
- Context does not grant a mission mandate. Models propose; responsible people retain authority over consequential acts.

The earlier five mandatory fit criteria and universal dismissal of solo use are withdrawn as working selection rules. Historical competitor claims are not refreshed or endorsed by this update.

## Choosing a first episode

Prefer an accessible participant, a recurring reconstruction or review problem, a useful output, public or sanitized sources, and a practical comparison with ordinary tools. One willing lead matters more than a large hypothetical audience. Begin where the result is useful before a crowd arrives.

[EXPERIMENT.md](EXPERIMENT.md) specifies a small, approximately ten-day trial: one lead, two contributors and a fresh reader, followed by a bounded public step only if the group loop works. These are proposed roles, not recruited people. The [Latvia case](experiments/LATVIA.md) supplies a concrete historical inquiry with source-linked seed records. It is a candidate, not a selected market or an allegation of failure.

Personal learning, maker projects, cultural work, investigative reporting and organizational handover remain plausible applications. We are not implementing them all. Their next-stage priority depends on observed benefit and the domain's operating requirements.

## Architecture and model implications

Keep sources, revisions, relationships, audiences, review decisions, work and receipts as explicit records. The main experience shows the current account, unresolved questions and useful ways to help; details expose sources and history. Chat is one input. Saved, reviewed and published are separate properties.

Build the first contribution-to-result episode with manual review and deterministic storage. General LLMs may draft an extraction, explanation or translation with references. Jev may later assist bounded routing or classification if task-specific evaluation shows a benefit. Neither decides truth, permission, expenditure or public release. Local model training and federation wait for a demonstrated need and the relevant data rights.

The matcher remains reusable for known needs. It does not yet implement general inquiry, durable permissioned knowledge or safe multi-project operation. Preserve C1–C9 and repair the relevant boundaries before operating those paths.

## Decisions still open

The real participant, chosen issue, owner of review, tolerable maintenance cost and sustainable operating model remain open. The inquiry experiment can reveal usefulness; one successful episode cannot establish representative public participation, long-term adoption or a business model.
