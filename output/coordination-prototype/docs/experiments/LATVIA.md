# Latvia: follow a public decision into an inspectable result

29 September 2026. Source research checked 28 September; this is a proposed test case, not an investigation completed with participants. Follow the [experiment protocol](../EXPERIMENT.md) and use the [pilot workbook](WORKBOOK.md) for the actual episode. No people have been contacted and no allegations are made.

## Recommended first question

**What can public evidence establish about delivery of the original “Sajūti Juglu” walking path, and what remains unverified?**

This is a retrospective inquiry into a bounded Riga participatory-budget project. A useful first result is a short, revisable explanation that distinguishes the original project from its later extension, planned costs from reported costs, and official reporting from independent observation. It tests institutional follow-through without assuming failure or wrongdoing.

## First contributions to invite within the pilot

1. Recover the original approved scope and acceptance documentation. Compare promised and reported features without inventing an omission.
2. Reconcile the reported cost with contract documents, tax treatment and any scope changes. The difference between two stated amounts is a question, not proof of misconduct.
3. If a willing participant can visit, check one feature with a dated observation and explicit limits. Do not infer a participant's health or political beliefs from their task.
4. Establish the extension's latest documented stage separately. Not finding an update in this search does not establish that nothing happened.

These are open tasks, not completed checks. If the original scope or contract is unavailable, that is a visible limitation in the result. Public project material does not authorize disclosure of contributors' identities or private notes.

## Wider political uses

| Question a person could follow | Sources to connect | Useful result and necessary distinction |
|---|---|---|
| What changed in this bill, and who supported each step? | [Saeima legislative records](https://www.saeima.lv/lv/likumdosana/likumdosanas-datu-baze) and the relevant sitting's recorded votes | Versioned explanation of text and procedure; committee referral, amendment votes and final passage must not be collapsed |
| What do parties actually propose on one issue? | [CVK candidate/programme entry point](https://www.cvk.lv/kandidatu-saraksti) and original published programmes | A sourced comparison on the same questions, with omissions and differences visible; a campaign statement is not an enacted measure |
| What happened after residents supported an initiative? | [ManaBalss process](https://manabalss.lv/pages/author-guide), institutional decisions, budgets and implementation evidence | Trace input to response and outcome; collecting support does not establish implementation |
| Where can people contribute before a decision? | [TAP participation notices](https://tapportals.mk.gov.lv/public_participation) | An understandable issue brief, missing perspectives and verified participation window; a draft is not current law |
| What do public money records establish? | [IUB procurement sources](https://www.iub.gov.lv/lv/dati) and [KNAB party finance records](https://info.knab.gov.lv/) | Separate spending and finance explanations with identifiers and dates; a relationship, donation or contract alone is not evidence of improper influence |

These are source entry points, not implemented connectors or guarantees of complete APIs. Recheck current records, access conditions and deadlines for each inquiry. CVK currently identifies the parliamentary election as 3 October 2026; keep a programme-comparison experiment bounded and preserve it for later follow-through rather than promising a comprehensive election product in days.

## Architectural lessons

Assign separate internal IDs to the original project and extension, joined by an explicit relationship. Keep event dates, document dates and retrieval dates separate. Preserve a permitted source snapshot when implemented; this starter account retains links and locators, not full downloaded evidence or independent review.

Separate what happened, what a source claims, how people interpret it, what they prefer and what a group authorizes. Let people choose issues and contributions, with common sourcing standards across viewpoints. Evidence can be unequal; equal treatment does not require treating every assertion as equally supported. Self-selected participants are not a representative electorate.

The product's useful political promise is continuity: someone can see the evidence behind a public issue, add a missing piece and see what changed. Generalized politician scores, inferred political profiles and a single model-generated verdict would obscure that promise.
