Saving confirms location and audience, not truth. Every disposition needs a reason. Acceptance names the changed revision or explains why no change was needed. A receipt being available is different from confirmed delivery or reading.

## Handover, challenge and decision

Without briefing, ask the fresh reader to explain the question, locate a finding's evidence and limits, distinguish the two projects, identify an open question and find a contribution's outcome. Record actual answers, assistance and the version shown.

For the challenge test, record the alternative explanation or contrary source sought, what was found, and whether it changed the account. An untested challenge remains untested; it does not establish consensus.

| Gate | Result | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|
| A contribution improves the account | Not tested | C-ID and before/after revision |
| Every submission gets a disposition | Not tested | Received/closed counts and outstanding reasons |
| Fresh reader finds state and evidence | Not tested | Answers and help required |
| Challenge handled transparently | Not tested | Source and resolution or preserved uncertainty |
| Upkeep is tolerable | Not tested | Time log, comparison conditions and participant judgment |
| Someone chooses another use | Not observed | Actual choice/action, separate from prompted testing |

**Decision: pending. Responsible person: unassigned.** Record continue, simplify, stop or inconclusive with evidence, limits and the next bounded action. A populated worksheet proves recordkeeping; actual participant observations are still required.
